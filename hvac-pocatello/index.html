Two situations call for quick action from you before a technician arrives. The first is an iced-over air conditioner: turn the system off at the thermostat and set the fan to "on" so warm indoor air thaws the coil, because continuing to run it can overflow the drain pan and damage the compressor. The second is a furnace that short-cycles or gives off a burning smell. Shut it down at the switch and call us. That small step can prevent a far bigger repair.

Keeping Your Pocatello HVAC System Running Longer

The best way to avoid a mid-season breakdown is simple maintenance. We recommend that homeowners replace filters every one to three months, keep vegetation trimmed two feet back from the outdoor condenser, rinse the condenser coil each spring, and have a professional tune-up twice a year, cooling in spring and heating in fall, so small problems get caught while they're still cheap.

Equipment age matters just as much. With regular service, a central air conditioner typically lasts 12 to 15 years, a furnace 15 to 20, and a heat pump 10 to 15. Once a system passes that range and starts showing warning signs, like rising energy bills, rooms that won't reach the setpoint, or repeat service calls, it's smart to start planning a replacement. Replacing on your own schedule costs less and is far less stressful than scrambling for a new system during the first heat wave of the year.

Small problems rarely stay small for long. Take a weak capacitor: swapping it out is a quick, affordable repair, but left alone for a season, it can take the compressor with it, and just like that a minor fix has become a full system replacement. That's why we encourage homeowners to keep our number in their phone and reach out at the first hint of trouble. Catching it early saves money and spares you a few miserable nights.
